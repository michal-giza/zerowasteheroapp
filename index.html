Information We Collect Personal Information Email Address: We collect your email address when you create an account. This is used solely for authentication purposes and to provide you with application services. Location Data GPS Location: When you perform waste collecting activities, we collect your location data to: Draw the area you've covered during your activity Calculate the distance you've walked Map your waste collection route Usage Data Analytics Information: We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the app experience. Crash Reports: We utilize Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports that help us identify and fix technical issues. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: Provide, maintain, and improve our application Create and manage your user account Calculate and display your waste collection statistics Map your collection routes and areas covered Analyze app performance and fix issues Display advertisements through AdMob Data Storage and Security All user data is stored in Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage). Within our database, all user data is associated with a unique user ID rather than your email address to provide an additional layer of privacy. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information: Firebase authentication for secure user access Database security rules restricting data access to authenticated users only Regular security audits and updates Third-Party Services Our app uses the following third-party services: Firebase (Firestore and Storage): For data storage and authentication Google Analytics: For app usage statistics Firebase Crashlytics: For crash reporting AdMob: For in-app advertisements Each of these services may collect information sent by your device as part of their functionality. This collection is governed by their respective privacy policies. Sharing Your Information We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your data is only processed within Firebase for the purposes of providing app functionality.
