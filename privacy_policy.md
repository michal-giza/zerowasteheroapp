# Privacy Policy for Zero Waste Hero App 2

**Last Updated: March 6, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Zero Waste Hero App 2 ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage within this mobile application, Zero Waste Hero App 2.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

### 2.1. Personal Information

*   **Email Address:** When you create an account, we collect your email address. This is used solely for account authentication and to provide you with access to the application services.

### 2.2. Location Data

*   **GPS Location:** When you engage in waste collecting activities within the app, we collect your GPS location data for the following purposes:
    *   To visually represent the area you have covered during your activity.
    *   To calculate the distance you have walked while collecting waste.
    *   To map your waste collection route for your records and app features.

### 2.3. Usage Data

*   **Analytics Information:** We utilize Google Analytics to gather anonymous usage statistics. This data helps us understand how users interact with the app and allows us to improve the overall user experience.
*   **Crash Reports:** We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports. These reports are essential for identifying and resolving technical issues and ensuring the stability of the app.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

*   Provide, maintain, and continuously improve the Zero Waste Hero App 2.
*   Create and effectively manage your user account.
*   Accurately calculate and display your waste collection statistics, such as distance and area covered.
*   Visually map your waste collection routes and areas you have cleaned.
*   Analyze application performance and diagnose and fix any technical issues or bugs.
*   Display advertisements within the app through AdMob to support the app's development and maintenance.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

All user data is securely stored using Firebase services (Firestore and Firebase Storage). To enhance your privacy, within our database, your data is linked to a unique user ID rather than directly to your email address.

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information:

*   **Firebase Authentication:** We use Firebase Authentication to ensure secure user access and account management.
*   **Database Security Rules:** We have configured Firebase database security rules to restrict data access to authenticated users only, preventing unauthorized access.
*   **Regular Security Audits and Updates:** We conduct regular security audits and implement updates to our systems to protect against potential vulnerabilities and threats.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates with the following third-party services to provide its functionality:

*   **Firebase (Firestore and Storage):** Used for secure data storage, database management, and user authentication.
*   **Google Analytics:** Used for collecting anonymized app usage statistics to improve app design and features.
*   **Firebase Crashlytics:** Used for automated crash reporting to quickly identify and resolve app errors.
*   **AdMob:** Used to display in-app advertisements, which helps support the app's free availability.

Please be aware that each of these third-party services may collect information sent by your device as part of their operational functionality. This data collection is governed by their respective privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

## 6. Sharing Your Information

We are committed to your privacy. **We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties for marketing or any other purposes.** Your data is solely processed within the Firebase environment for the purpose of providing and improving the functionality of Zero Waste Hero App 2.

## 7. Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

*   **Access your data:** You have the ability to view the information we have collected about you directly within the app.
*   **Delete your data:** You can completely remove all data associated with your account by using the "Logout and Erase Data" function available within the app's settings.
*   **Request data information:** For any privacy-related concerns or to request more detailed information about your data, please contact us at [zerowasteheroapp.team@gmail.com](mailto:zerowasteheroapp.team@gmail.com).

## 8. Children's Privacy

Zero Waste Hero App 2 does not have specific age restrictions. However, if you are under the age of 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), we strongly recommend that you use the app with the guidance and consent of your parent or legal guardian.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will notify you by posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page and revising the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy regularly for any updates.
